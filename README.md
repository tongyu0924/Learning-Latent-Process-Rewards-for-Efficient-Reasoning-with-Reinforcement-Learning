# Learning Latent Process Rewards for Efficient Reasoning with Reinforcement Learning

Outcome-reward RL on the continuous latent transitions of a latent-recurrent LM ([Huginn-3.5B](https://huggingface.co/tomg-group-umd/huginn-0125)).
No external LLM grader and no latent-to-text decoding: the verifiable signal is whether the final answer is correct, optionally complemented by a learned scorer that reads the latent refinement states.

- Method: surrogate latent policy from SLPO ([arXiv:2607.19691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19691))
- Latent scorer: adapted from SWIFT ([arXiv:2505.12225](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12225)), with refinement states in place of layers
- Experimental setup follows LSRL ([Findings of EMNLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.669.pdf))

## Algorithm

### Notation

- $x$: problem, $a$: gold answer
- $y_i = (y_{i,1},\dots,y_{i,n_i})$: tokens generated by rollout $i$
- At every token position $t$, the model runs $r$ latent transitions:

$$
h_{t,k} = R_\theta\big(e_t,\ h_{t,k-1};\ m_{t,k}\big), \qquad k = 1,\dots,r
$$

$$
y_t \sim p_\theta(\cdot \mid h_{t,r})
$$

  - $R_\theta$: recurrent core (the only place LoRA is applied)
  - $e_t$: prelude output
  - $m_{t,k}$: MC-dropout mask ($p = 0.1$), the source of stochasticity in the latent policy
- $z_i = \{h_{t,k}\}$: all latents realized during the rollout, stored as stop-gradient targets

### 1. Reward

$$
r_i = \mathbf 1\big[\text{Answer}(y_i) = a\big]
$$

$$
r'_i = r_i - \lambda \frac{T_i}{T_{\max}}, \qquad T_i = n_i \cdot r
$$

$T_i$ is the number of latent steps used by rollout $i$, and $\lambda$ is the step cost.

### 2. Advantage

$$
A_{i,t} = r'_i - b_{i,t}
$$

Two choices of baseline $b_{i,t}$:

**RLOO**

$$
b_{i,t} = \frac{1}{G-1} \sum_{j \ne i} r'_j
$$

**Latent value**

$$
b_{i,t} = V_\phi\big(\mathrm{sg}(h_{i,t-1,r})\big)
$$

$$
\min_\phi \sum_{i,t} \big(V_\phi(h_{i,t-1,r}) - r'_i\big)^2
$$

$V_\phi$ only sees latents before position $t$, so it is an unbiased baseline. It is used only for variance reduction and never added to the reward.

### 3. Surrogate policy

Given the realized previous latent, re-run the core with $K$ dropout masks to form an empirical Gaussian:

$$
z^{(j)}_{t,k} = R_\theta\big(e_t,\ \mathrm{sg}(h_{t,k-1});\ m^{(j)}\big), \qquad j = 1,\dots,K
$$

$$
\mu_{t,k} = \frac1K \sum_j z^{(j)}_{t,k}
$$

$$
\sigma^2_{t,k} = \frac1K \sum_j \big(z^{(j)}_{t,k} - \mu_{t,k}\big)^2 + \epsilon
$$

Surrogate log-likelihood at position $t$ (the latent term is averaged over hidden dimensions):

$$
\ell_t(\theta) = \sum_{k=1}^{r} \log \mathcal N\big(h_{t,k};\ \mu_{t,k},\ \sigma^2_{t,k}\big) + \log p_\theta\big(y_t \mid \mu_{t,r}\big)
$$

### 4. Update

$$
\mathcal L(\theta) = -\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i} \frac{1}{n_i} \sum_{t=1}^{n_i} \mathrm{sg}(A_{i,t})\ \ell_{i,t}(\theta)
$$

That is, $\nabla_\theta J \approx A \cdot \nabla_\theta \log \tilde\pi_\theta(z, y \mid x)$.

### 5. Latent scorer

For the position that predicts answer token $j$, concatenate the $r$ refinement states:

$$
u_{i,j} = \big[h_{i,j,1};\ \dots;\ h_{i,j,r}\big] \in \mathbb R^{rd}
$$

A single linear map $W \in \mathbb R^{2 \times rd}$ gives a gate and a score per token:

$$
\tilde g_{i,j} = w_g^\top u_{i,j} + b_g, \qquad s_{i,j} = w_s^\top u_{i,j} + b_s, \qquad g_{i,j} = \sigma(\tilde g_{i,j})
$$

Gated average over answer positions:

$$
S_i = \frac{\sum_j g_{i,j}\, s_{i,j}}{\max\big(\sum_j g_{i,j},\ \epsilon\big)}
$$

Trained on detached states with final-answer labels, on GSM8K problems disjoint from the RL subset:

$$
c_i = \mathbf 1\big[\text{Answer}(y_i) = a\big], \qquad \mathcal L_{\text{score}} = \mathrm{BCEWithLogits}(S_i,\ c_i)
$$

The scorer is then frozen and used in two ways:

- **Best-of-N:** sample $N$ answers and return $\arg\max_i S_i$. The RL reward is unchanged.
- **Extra reward:** add the scorer's prediction to the verifiable reward:

$$
R'_i = c_i - \lambda \frac{n_i}{N_{\max}} + \eta\, \sigma(S_i)
$$

### Loop

```
train latent scorer on base-model rollouts (detached states, BCE on correctness), then freeze it

for each batch (32 problems, G = 8 rollouts each):
    rollout with dropout on; store y and every h_{t,k}
    r' = 1[correct] − λ·T/T_max   (+ η·σ(S) for the scorer variant)
    A  = r' − b                 # b = RLOO or V_φ
    for k = 1..r:
        re-run core K times from sg(h_{t,k−1}) → μ, σ²
        loss −= A · log N(h_{t,k}; μ, σ²)     (at k = r also add A · log p(y_t | μ))
        backward
    update V_φ with MSE
    AdamW step on core LoRA
```

Evaluation uses greedy decoding with dropout off, and reports pass@1 and average latent steps $\bar T$.

## Experiments

`slpo_value_cost` is the full method. The other rows are ablations that each remove one component, so the effect of each part can be measured separately.

| Variant | Role | Update | Baseline | Step cost $\lambda$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `outcome` | token-level RL baseline | token-level GRPO (clip + KL) | group mean | – |
| `slpo` | ablation: no value, no cost | surrogate latent policy | RLOO | – |
| `slpo_value` | ablation: no cost | surrogate latent policy | $V_\phi$ | – |
| **`slpo_value_cost`** | **full method** | surrogate latent policy | $V_\phi$ | **0.1** |
| `slpo_value_cost_scorer` | extension: + latent scorer reward | surrogate latent policy | $V_\phi$ | 0.1, $\eta = 0.2$ |

Ablation-only variants (same reward as the full method, one component swapped):

| Variant | Update | Baseline | Step cost $\lambda$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| `outcome_cost` | token-level GRPO | group mean | 0.1 |
| `slpo_cost` | surrogate latent policy | RLOO | 0.1 |

Reading the table: `slpo` vs `outcome` isolates latent-level updates, `slpo_value` vs `slpo` isolates the value baseline, `slpo_value_cost` vs `slpo_value` isolates the step cost (compare both accuracy and latent steps), and `slpo_value_cost_scorer` vs `slpo_value_cost` isolates the scorer reward.

Latent scorer evaluation:

- **Held-out validation:** AUC and BCE on 200 GSM8K train problems not used elsewhere.
- **Best-of-8 on GSM8K test:** scorer pick vs. majority vote vs. mean sample accuracy vs. oracle (any correct). Run on the base model and after `slpo_value_cost` / `slpo_value_cost_scorer`.

Shared settings:

- Training data: 500 random GSM8K train problems
- $r = 8$, $G = 8$, $K = 4$, dropout $p = 0.1$
- LoRA rank 8 / α 16, core only
- lr = 2e-6, 32 problems per update
- Scorer: 1000 GSM8K train problems × 8 rollouts, 2 epochs, AdamW (lr 1e-4, wd 1e-5)

Evaluated on GSM8K, MathQA and MATH, with SFT-r8 / SFT-r32 as references.

Colab: Runtime → Change runtime type → **A100** or **L4** GPU.

```bash
!git clone https://github.com/tongyu0924/Learning-Latent-Process-Rewards-for-Efficient-Reasoning-with-Reinforcement-Learning
%cd Learning-Latent-Process-Rewards-for-Efficient-Reasoning-with-Reinforcement-Learning

!python train.py --preset quick                       # smoke test (a few minutes)
!python train.py                                      # full method, Colab-sized run (~2 h on A100)
!python train.py --ablation all                       # full method + the 3 ablations
!python train.py --ablation cost                      # one ablation: latent | value | cost
!python train.py --eval_limit 0                       # evaluate on the full test sets
!python train.py --preset paper                       # original settings, all variants (many GPU hours)
!python train.py --preset paper --scorer_path outputs/latent_scorer.pt  # reuse a trained scorer
```

Add `--out_dir /content/drive/MyDrive/slpo` (after mounting Drive) so results survive a disconnect.

Every run prints three stages: (1) evaluate the untrained base model, (2) RL training, (3) evaluate the trained model.
At the end it prints the results table next to the paper baselines and, with `--ablation`, the paired difference for each ablation.

| | `colab` (default) | `paper` |

The `colab` changes exist so a short run actually learns: with LoRA starting at $B = 0$, lr 2e-6 barely moves the weights in 32 updates, and at $r = 8$ the base model solves ~15% of GSM8K, so with $T = 1.0$ plus dropout on random problems most rollout groups are all-wrong and give no signal.
During training, held-out greedy accuracy on 100 GSM8K test problems (disjoint from the reported 300) is logged as `mon_acc` every 8 updates.

Outputs go to `outputs/`:

- `results.json` (including `ablations`)
- `train_*.json`: training logs
- `eval_*.jsonl`: evaluation records
- `bon_*.jsonl`: best-of-N records

## Notes

- **Fixed depth:** Huginn's depth is fixed at $r$, so $T_i \propto n_i$ and the step cost acts as a length penalty. SLPO's adaptive stopping head is not implemented.
- **Dropout:** Huginn was pretrained without dropout, so keep $p$ small.
- **Token term uses $\mu_{t,r}$:** LoRA lives only in the core, so the token term uses the recomputed $\mu_{t,r}$ to let gradients reach it.
- **Large λ:** a large $\lambda$ encourages stopping too early; check acc and steps together.
- **Scorer reward:** $S_i$ scores the whole trajectory from its latents; it is not a reliable per-refinement correctness score. The policy can learn to please the scorer, so validate the scorer (AUC, best-of-N) before trusting the `slpo_value_cost_scorer` results, and keep $\eta$ small.
- **Scorer distribution shift:** the scorer is trained on base-model states. Half of its training batches use MC-dropout so it also sees RL-style rollouts, but LoRA updates still shift the states during RL.
